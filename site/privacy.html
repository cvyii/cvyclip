THE WEBSITE, EXPLAINED

Website privacy.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This note describes the CvyClip AI landing website as delivered. CvyClip AI is a project in development. This site introduces the idea and includes an illustrative concept preview.

What this website collects

The website code has no sign-up form, email collection, payment service, analytics, or advertising trackers. It doesn’t ask you to upload a video or create an account.

What happens in the preview

Changing a format or caption style updates a sample illustration in your browser. Your choices aren’t sent to a server or saved after you leave or reload the page. No AI model is called and no video is processed.

Cookies and browser storage

The website code doesn’t set cookies or use local storage. Fonts, illustrations, styles, and scripts are included with the site, with no third-party embeds or font requests.

Hosting and technical requests

Your browser requests the pages and assets from the service hosting this website. The hosting provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, and browser details, and may keep logs under its own policies. Those hosting activities are separate from this website’s code.

Future changes

If video uploads, accounts, analytics, or other data-handling features are introduced, this note will need to be updated before those features go live. This note doesn’t describe a future video processing service.
